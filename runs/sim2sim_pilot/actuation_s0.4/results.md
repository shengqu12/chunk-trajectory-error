## Phase C

- Seeds [17, 18, 19, 20, 21]. Per seed a base policy (`02_prepare_policy.py` unchanged, per-seed config), then fine-tunes from it with the 06 recipe (5000 steps, lr 3e-4, batch 256, identical demonstration batch sequence per seed).
- Methods: zero-shot, imitation-selected, gap-demo, fine-tune, smoothness, magnitude. gap-logged is dropped (amendment 12). G = the sqrtJ network of this Phase B (`checkpoints/gap_sqrtJ.pt`), trained once from pi_bc's actuation-target rollouts and reused frozen for all seeds (amendment 18).
- Magnitude penalty: beta * mean over the H chunk steps and the 3 translation dims of the squared policy command (same scope and reduction as the smoothness penalty).
- Selection: every distinct candidate checkpoint runs in the target from the 100 val-split seeds (200-step cap); most successes wins, ties to the smaller weight / earlier checkpoint. Final evaluation: seeds 300000-300149 (never used before), target and source, one 400-step run per episode: success@200 (primary), success@400 (secondary).
- Intervals: two-way cluster bootstrap (5000 resamples) over seeds and initial states (true gap: seeds and logged episodes); 95% percentile intervals.

Checks: imitation-selected's step-5000 checkpoint is bitwise the zero-shot policy for seeds [17, 18, 19, 20, 21] (it reuses zero-shot's selection rollouts); the magnitude trainer reproduces 06's smoothness fine-tune bitwise when given the smoothness term for seeds [17, 18, 19, 20, 21].

## Notes

- Zero-shot has a single candidate; every other method (including smoothness and magnitude, which use no target data for training) is selected on target rollouts. Imitation-selected isolates that selection effect for the imitation objective.
- Ratio of the weighted auxiliary gradient norm to the imitation gradient norm at step 5000 (mean over seeds): gap-demo 0.0001: 0.10; gap-demo 0.0003: 0.25; gap-demo 0.001: 0.48; gap-demo 0.003: 0.65; gap-demo 0.01: 0.78; smoothness 0.1: 0.05; smoothness 0.3: 0.11; smoothness 1: 0.30; magnitude 0.1: 0.44; magnitude 0.3: 0.76; magnitude 1: 0.92. Gap-demo's smallest lambda already contributes about a tenth of the imitation gradient, while val BC loss is almost unchanged (`training.json`).
- gap-demo selected the lower edge of the lambda grid on 4 of 5 seeds and magnitude the lower edge of its beta grid on 5; the grids were not changed after seeing this.
- Target mean steps and mean EE step are over the 400-step episodes (failures run to 400).
- G is trained once, from pi_bc's rollouts (seed-17 lineage), and reused for every seed.
- The true gap of each policy's own chunk and both gate-3 tests are sim-to-sim-only measurements (target restored); none is available on hardware.
